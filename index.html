FOUNDER / CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Rohini Soedhwa

Music Producer · Songwriter · Vocalist · Sound Designer · Interdisciplinary Artist

Hement Productions was founded by Rohini Soedhwa, also known through her artist work as Tita Boku. Born in Paramaribo, Suriname and now based in British Columbia, Rohini’s background and wide-ranging musical influences have shaped an instinct for experimentation, encouraging a practice that moves freely between genres, technologies and forms of expression.

Her work moves between experimental electronic music, pop, hip-hop, collaborative performance and visual culture, combining instinctive songwriting and vocal work with layered electronic production, sound design and a technically driven approach to creative practice.

Hement extends that practice into an evolving creative house for music, interdisciplinary work and emerging interactive worlds — with indie game development, world-building and new forms of digital storytelling forming part of its next chapter.

Education BFA in Electroacoustic Music — Simon Fraser University, School for the Contemporary Arts
Practice Music Production · Songwriting · Sound Design · Live Performance · Creative Technology
Based British Columbia, Canada
